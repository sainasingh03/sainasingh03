## 🌷 Featured Projects

### 1. NagarDrishti AI --- Pothole & Civic Road Safety

**Focus:** Computer vision · Civic technology · Road safety

-   A concept for identifying potholes, road cracks, and waterlogging
    from road images.
-   Designed around hazard mapping, safer route awareness, and keeping
    resolved hazards off the active map.
-   Intended to help organize road-condition evidence for civic
    improvement.

### 2. SATYAAI --- ISRO SatQuery AI

**Focus:** Earth observation · Satellite imagery · AI-assisted insights

-   An Earth-observation project concept for exploring satellite data
    and understanding environmental change.
-   Designed around asking questions about Earth imagery and presenting
    useful geospatial context.
-   **Tagline:** *Ask the Earth. Understand the Change.*

### 3. AgriMind --- Intelligent Irrigation using MDP

**Focus:** Sustainable agriculture · Decision systems · Cloud
application

-   Uses a Markov Decision Process (MDP) approach to reason about
    irrigation decisions from conditions such as soil moisture,
    temperature, humidity, and rain probability.
-   Goal: support more efficient, sustainable, and data-driven
    irrigation.
-   Explores how changing weather conditions can affect irrigation
    decisions.

### 4. ResQAI --- AI Disaster Response Copilot

**Focus:** Disaster response · AI · Resource mapping

-   An AI-assisted disaster-response concept focused on supporting
    situational awareness and resource mapping.
-   Developed for **Build with Bharat 2.0**; team reached the
    **semifinalist** stage.

### 5. EduBridge --- Remote Classroom for Rural Colleges

**Focus:** EdTech · Accessibility · Low-connectivity learning

-   A Smart India Hackathon project concept for improving access to
    remote learning.
-   Proposed features include live/recorded classes, offline-friendly
    learning, translation, speech-to-text, adaptive quizzes, and a
    teacher dashboard.
-   **Role:** Team Lead; internally selected for SIH.

### 6. VeriQuant --- Fake News Detection

**Focus:** NLP · Machine Learning · Misinformation awareness

-   A project exploring how NLP and ML can help identify potentially
    misleading news content.
-   Selected for an internal SIH process in January 2025.

### 7. Sales Boomer --- Fake Sales Detection

**Focus:** RAG · LLMs · NLP · Web application

-   An academic project exploring signals that may indicate misleading
    sales claims.
-   Envisions combining information retrieval and language models with a
    clear, user-friendly interface.

### 8. SATARK --- Investor Safety Companion

**Focus:** Scam awareness · OCR · Risk signals · RAG

-   A concept for helping users examine suspicious investment messages
    and links.
-   Planned ideas include screenshot/text extraction, URL checks,
    risk-signal explanations, and accessible Hindi/Hinglish guidance.

> Project status and feature maturity may vary. Please check the linked
> repositories for the latest implementation and documentation.
